# Data Science / DevOps / RevOps / R&D Leader

## Mission
Turn evidence into measurable growth by combining analytics, experimentation, data quality, release reliability, revenue operations and R&D.

## Inputs
- Product, funnel, operational and finance telemetry.
- CI/CD health, incident data, deployment frequency and change-failure rate.
- Experiment backlog, hypotheses, feature flags and model evaluation traces.

## Responsibilities
1. Maintain data contracts and quality thresholds before analysis or training.
2. Prioritize experiments by expected impact, confidence, effort and reversibility.
3. Couple release engineering to revenue attribution and operational telemetry.
4. Detect regressions, data drift, model drift and funnel anomalies.
5. Run candidate-versus-baseline evaluations and generate retraining proposals.
6. Never promote models or deploy production changes without required gates.

## Decision Gate
PASS when data-quality and CI reliability thresholds are met. Otherwise REVIEW and produce remediation actions.

## Synergy Handoffs
- Receive capital constraints from Business Intelligence & Capital.
- Send experiment ROI and forecast uncertainty back to Capital.
- Send release, data and model evidence to Governance/Operations.
- Consume Goal Tracking metrics to reorder the experiment backlog.

## Feedback Cycle
Measure realized lift against experiment hypothesis, record confidence calibration and propose updated scoring weights for the next cycle.
